# Canva Design Feedback

## Instructions
1. Review the design for visual hierarchy, contrast, legibility, composition, and message clarity.
2. Tie feedback to the brief objective and target audience.
3. Give prioritized, actionable notes (P0 blockers → P2 polish), not vague opinions.
4. Keep brand guardrails as hard constraints, aesthetics as soft.

## Examples
- "Critique the IG story `DAF-777`" → note CTA visibility, text safe-area, focal point.

## Performance Notes
- Lead with the 2-3 highest-impact fixes; avoid nitpick lists.

## Troubleshooting
- If the brief is unclear, request/annotate the missing objective before critiquing.
