# Brief Writer

## Instructions
1. Ground every brief in `CONTEXT/CONTEXT_BRIEF.md`; never invent brand facts, prices, or claims.
2. One brief per channel × scenario that exists in `SCENARIOS/` and `MATRIX/PRODUCTION_GRID.csv`.
3. Include: objective, audience, single key message, mandatory brand elements, size/spec, CTA, deliverables.
4. Mark missing inputs `araştırılacak / owner tarafından doldurulacak`.

## Examples
- "Write the IG story brief for Scenario 2" → complete, copy-paste-ready brief in `BRIEFS/`.

## Performance Notes
- One key message per brief; do not stack multiple asks into one asset.

## Troubleshooting
- Guardrail/spec conflict → note it in the brief; guardrail and spec win over creative wishlist.
